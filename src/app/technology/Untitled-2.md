

# 1. AssistPro AI

### Project Title
**AssistPro AI – AI-Powered Productivity & Task Automation Platform**

### Project Link
https://assistpro.ai

### Description
AssistPro AI is an intelligent productivity platform that helps individuals and teams manage tasks, reminders, documents, and schedules through natural language conversations. Users can interact via WhatsApp, Telegram, or a web dashboard, while AI automatically organizes tasks, processes documents, generates reminders, and streamlines day-to-day productivity workflows.

# 2. UserCall

### Project Title
**UserCall – AI-Powered Voice Research Platform**

### Project Link
https://www.usercall.co

### Description
UserCall enables businesses to conduct AI-moderated voice interviews at scale without manual scheduling or moderation. The platform automatically transcribes conversations, identifies themes, analyzes sentiment, and generates actionable insights, helping product, UX, and marketing teams make faster, data-driven decisions.
